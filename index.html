02Presenting symptoms

Most ad accounts aren't sick. They're unattended.

It's not a talent problem. It's a scheduling problem. Four symptoms of the usual setup — and what Dr. PPC does about each one.

Symptom 01The monthly-meeting cadence

Your account changes by the hour. Someone looks at it properly on a call once a month — and the retainer gets paid either way.

TreatmentRounds, every single day

Every campaign, keyword, search term and placement read daily, reconciled against a full attribution window. Thirty rounds a month, not one call.

Symptom 02Managed to ACoS optics

Bids tuned to make a percentage look good on a chart. The campaign with the prettier ACoS isn't always the one paying you.

TreatmentA goal per product, judged on profit

Profitability, growth or a balance, set per ASIN — and managed to contribution profit, not to how the ACoS column looks.

Symptom 03Bids that haven't moved since spring

Set once, tuned once, then left. The auction moved on. The bids didn't.

TreatmentSmall moves, daily, with a reason

Bids, budgets, negatives and placements adjusted as the auction moves — each with its evidence, a measurement window and a rollback trigger.

Symptom 04Vacations, sick days, turnover

Search terms don't take a week off. The person watching them does — and when they leave, the reasons behind every bid leave with them.

TreatmentOn shift 24/7/365 — and it's written down

No gaps, no handover. Every reason lives in the log, so the context stays with your account.

Visuals are illustrative, drawn from a demo account.The fix isn't a smarter person once a month. It's the same discipline, every day.
