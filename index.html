Your call / 04

You choose how much he does on his own.

"Autonomous" is not one setting, and it should never be the vendor's decision. Dr. PPC runs at whichever of these three levels you pick — and you can change it any time, per account, without renegotiating anything. Some brands start at Level 1 for 60 days, read the log, and move up. Some never do. Both are fine.

Level 01

Full human approval

Dr. PPC does all the analysis and proposes every change. Nothing touches Amazon without a human clicking approve.

  • Every bid, negative, budget and placement change arrives as a proposal card
  • Each card carries the evidence, the measurement plan and the rollback trigger
  • Approve, reject, or approve in bulk — one click each
  • Rejections are read back into the strategy, so he stops proposing what you keep refusing
The bid change aboveSits in the queue until you click "Approve and apply to Amazon".
Level 02

Supervised autonomy

The routine, low-risk work executes on its own inside guardrails you set. Anything above your threshold queues for approval.

  • Auto-applies: negatives on zero-order terms, bid steps within your band, search-term harvesting
  • Queues for you: anything above the threshold, structural changes, new campaigns
  • You set the band — for example, bid moves up to 10% and budget shifts up to $250/day
  • Daily digest of what ran automatically, with the same evidence attached
The bid change aboveAn 8% step inside a 10% band — applied automatically, logged, still reversible.
Level 03

Fully autonomous

Dr. PPC runs the account inside agreed guardrails. You review the log and the outcomes rather than each decision.

  • Every change is still written down with its reasoning and its evidence
  • Every change still carries a measurement window and a rollback trigger
  • Guardrails are yours: target profitability, spend ceilings, protected SKUs, untouchable campaigns
  • Weekly written account update; drop back to Level 1 at any moment

What does not change between levels

The same evidence trail

Level 3 is not a quieter product. Every change produces the identical written record — you're choosing whether to read it before or after.

The same rollback triggers

Nothing is applied without a condition that undoes it. Autonomy raises the speed, not the risk tolerance.

The same hard limits

The list below is off-limits at every level, including Level 3. Full autonomy does not mean unlimited authority.

The same switch, both ways

Move up when you trust it, drop back the week your supply chain gets interesting. Month-to-month, so there's nothing to renegotiate.

Questions / 10

The things people actually ask.

What happens in the first 30 days?

Dr. PPC diagnoses the account, writes the strategy per product, fixes what's obviously broken, and starts the daily loop. Most brands start at Level 1 so they can read every proposal before it ships. You see the weekly report and the full change log. You pay nothing. If it hasn't earned the second month by day 30, we shake hands and part ways.

Can I keep approval on everything?

Yes — that's Level 1, and plenty of accounts stay there. Dr. PPC still does all the analysis and writes every proposal; he just waits for your click before anything reaches Amazon. You can move to supervised or full autonomy later, or move back down, at any time and without renegotiating your agreement.

If I go fully autonomous, what stops a bad day?

Three things. Guardrails you set before he starts — target profitability, spend ceilings, protected SKUs. A rollback trigger attached to every individual change, so a move that doesn't perform is reversed automatically inside its measurement window. And the hard limits in the ladder above, which apply at every level: inventory risk, pricing, new launches and creative always come to a human, no matter what setting you're on.

Is Orbit really included?

Yes. The full suite — sales, advertising, search terms, keywords, traffic, inventory, finance, ASIN profitability, the BSR, buy box, price and fee trackers, and the Dr PPC Console itself — comes with the service at no additional charge. There is no separate software line on your invoice and no tier you have to buy up into to see your own numbers.

Is this just a bid rule with a marketing name?

No. A rules engine executes conditions you wrote. Fable 5 reads the whole account, writes a strategy per product against your economics, then acts on it — and logs the reasoning for every change so you can audit the thinking, not just the outcome.

How do I know the ads caused the growth?

Honestly: last-click attribution can't prove incrementality, and never could. What works is holding out a region or an ASIN set, comparing against a matched control, and reconciling in Amazon Marketing Cloud where DSP and sponsored ads stop double-counting. We'll show you the method before we show you a number.

Is my account big enough?

Dr. PPC fits brands doing roughly $500k a year and up on Amazon. Below that, the percentage is small enough that we probably aren't the highest-leverage thing you could buy — and we'll say so on the call.

Do I lose control of my account?

No. You keep ownership of everything, you choose the approval level, you can see every change and its reason, and the ladder above is the real boundary. Anything outside it comes to a human first.

What does the 3% cap mean?

The percentage stops growing past the cap no matter how large your ad spend gets. Scaling your business shouldn't quietly scale our invoice.
