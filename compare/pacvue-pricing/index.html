Pacvue Pricing: What You Can Actually Find Out Before the Call

Pacvue does not publish pricing. Its pricing URL returns a 404 and every route leads to a demo request. Third-party estimates commonly cite a $500/month minimum or roughly 2–4% of ad spend, whichever is greater, but Pacvue has not confirmed those figures publicly.

You searched "pacvue pricing" because Pacvue would not tell you

That is the honest reason this page exists, and it is worth naming. Pacvue's site has no pricing page in the navigation. The URL people link to, pacvue.com/pricing, returns a 404. Every product page ends in Book a Demo.

This is a deliberate enterprise sales motion, not an oversight. Pacvue sells to brands and agencies whose deals are negotiated on seat counts, retailer coverage and spend volume, and a published number would either be wrong for most buyers or would anchor the negotiation. Plenty of serious software companies work this way.

It still leaves you unable to answer a simple internal question — roughly, what is this going to cost? — without spending an hour on a call and handing over your spend figures first.

What third parties estimate, and how much to trust it

The commonly repeated structure is a floor of around $500 a month or a percentage of monthly ad spend, whichever is greater, with the percentage usually described as somewhere in the 2–4% range and rising for DSP and advanced modules. Larger enterprise accounts are frequently described in the $3,000–$5,000+ per month band.

Take all of that as directional. None of it comes from Pacvue. It comes from review aggregators, procurement marketplaces and agency blogs, each reporting what a handful of customers said, sometimes years ago. Software pricing pages we checked for other vendors in this category had already moved on from the figures the roundups were still quoting, in one case into a different currency.

The practical use of these estimates is not budgeting. It is knowing whether you are in the right ballpark to bother taking the call.

What actually drives your Pacvue quote

Five variables come up consistently, and knowing them lets you shape the conversation instead of reacting to it.

  • Monthly ad spend under management. The single biggest input, and the one they will ask for first.
  • Retailer coverage. Amazon only is one price. Amazon plus Walmart, Target, Instacart and TikTok Shop is another.
  • Modules. Commerce operations, digital shelf and intelligence are typically separate from media management.
  • Seats. Agencies with many operators pay differently from a three-person brand team.
  • Term. Annual is the norm at this level; multi-year usually moves the rate.

Ask for the quote broken out by module and by retailer. A single blended number is very hard to compare against anything, which is part of why it gets presented that way.

Pacvue is a serious platform, and some readers belong there

We are not going to pretend otherwise. Pacvue's own site says it covers more than 100 retailers across 30+ countries and that the five largest media agencies use it. Whatever the exact numbers, the category position is real: for cross-retailer commerce media at genuine enterprise scale, Pacvue is one of a very short list of credible answers.

If you are a large brand running media across Amazon, Walmart, Target, Instacart and TikTok Shop, with a procurement process, an in-house media team, and a need for one console the whole organisation reports out of, that is what Pacvue is built for and you should evaluate it properly. The same is true if you are an agency operating dozens of client accounts and need seat-level permissions and consolidated reporting.

Where it fits badly is the brand doing $50,000 a month on Amazon with nobody whose full-time job is running the console. Enterprise software without an enterprise operator is a subscription, not a capability.

Common questions

How much does Pacvue cost?

Pacvue does not publish a price. Third-party sources commonly describe a floor near $500/month or a percentage of ad spend in the 2–4% range, whichever is greater, with enterprise accounts often cited between $3,000 and $5,000+ per month. Pacvue has not confirmed these publicly.

Why does pacvue.com/pricing not work?

The URL returns a 404 and there is no pricing page in the site navigation. Pacvue routes all pricing questions through its sales team, which is normal for enterprise commerce media platforms.

Does Pacvue have a free trial?

No public free trial is advertised. The entry point is a booked demo. If you want hands-on time before committing, ask specifically for a sandbox or a paid pilot with defined exit terms.

Is Pacvue worth it for an Amazon-only brand?

Often not. Pacvue's strongest argument is breadth across many retailers. If Amazon is your whole business and nobody on your team will be in the platform daily, you are paying for coverage and seats you will not use.

What should I ask on the Pacvue demo?

Get the quote itemised by module, by retailer and by seat. Ask for the minimum term, the renewal uplift, and what happens to the fee when your spend drops in a slow quarter. A blended monthly number is the hardest thing to compare against alternatives.
