BidX Pricing: What the Vendor Actually Publishes

BidX publishes its prices. Its own pricing page lists Self Service Platform at €495/month, Managed Platform at €1,995/month and Managed Service at €4,995/month, each plus a percentage of connected ad spend, on an annual commitment. Third-party posts still quote older dollar figures that no longer match.

The current BidX price list

Three tiers for Amazon Sponsored Ads, taken from BidX's own pricing page rather than a roundup.

  • Self Service Platform — €495/month plus a percentage of connected ad spend. The platform, a growth manager, platform operations, and a monthly strategy call.
  • Managed Platform — €1,995/month plus a percentage. Adds up to two strategy calls a month and up to ten hours of assistance.
  • Managed Service — €4,995/month plus a percentage. Up to four strategy calls a month, up to 32 hours of assistance, full team support.

Amazon DSP is a separate line: €495/month self-service or €995/month managed, each plus a percentage. Amazon Marketing Cloud default reports are €295/month, with custom reports quoted on request. Share of Voice gives you the first 100 keywords free and charges $250/month per additional 500 keywords.

Two things the comparison posts get wrong

The currency. BidX is a German company and its pricing page quotes euros. A number of third-party articles still list $295, $1,500 and $3,500. Those are older figures in a different currency, and none of the three match what the vendor publishes today. If you built a budget off a listicle, open the source page before you sign anything.

The commitment. BidX sells on an annual commitment paid monthly. Several roundups describe a 14-day free trial; BidX's own page describes a three-month paid proof of concept for new customers instead. A twelve-month term is a normal enterprise arrangement, but it is a different purchase from a monthly subscription and it belongs in the comparison rather than in a footnote.

Neither error is malicious. Pricing pages change and roundups do not get re-checked. It is a good argument for reading the vendor's page yourself, including ours.

Three numbers to get in writing before you sign

1. The percentage. The variable rate on connected ad spend is not stated as a number anywhere on the pricing page. That is the figure that moves most as you scale, and it is the difference between a predictable software line and an open-ended one. Ask what it is, ask whether it tiers down at higher spend, and ask what counts as connected spend.

2. The term and the exit. Annual commitment, monthly payments — so ask what happens if you want out at month four, and whether the three-month paid proof of concept credits against the first year.

3. What is excluded. DSP, AMC reporting and Share of Voice are all priced separately. A quote for Sponsored Ads only is not a quote for your program. Add the modules you will actually turn on, then compare totals.

Where BidX is genuinely the right buy

This is a real product with real engineering behind it, and there are sellers who should buy it. If you have an in-house PPC person who wants a powerful console to drive, BidX gives them one. Its European coverage is a genuine strength — most US-built Amazon tools treat the EU marketplaces as an afterthought, and BidX does not.

The Managed Service tier is also honest about what it is. Four strategy calls and 32 hours of team time a month is people work at a people price, and €4,995 is inside the normal range for that. Judge that tier against agencies, not against software.

Where it gets harder is the middle. If you are paying €1,995 plus a percentage for ten hours a month of assistance, you are paying agency-adjacent money for tool-adjacent involvement. That is the tier worth pressure-testing.

Common questions

How much does BidX cost per month?

BidX's published Sponsored Ads tiers are €495, €1,995 and €4,995 per month, each plus an unstated percentage of connected ad spend. DSP, AMC reporting and Share of Voice are priced on top.

Why do other sites list BidX at $295 a month?

Those are older figures in dollars. The vendor's current page quotes euros at €495 for the entry tier. Roundups rarely get re-checked when a pricing page changes, which is why it is worth opening the source.

Does BidX have a free trial?

BidX's own page describes a three-month paid proof of concept for new customers. Some third-party articles mention a 14-day free trial. Confirm which applies to you before assuming a no-cost evaluation.

Is BidX worth it for a smaller seller?

At €495 a month plus a percentage on an annual commitment, the entry tier is a meaningful fixed cost. It makes most sense when you have someone in-house who will actually use the console daily. If nobody owns PPC internally, you are buying a tool no one drives.
