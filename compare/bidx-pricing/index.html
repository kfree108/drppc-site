The current BidX price list

Three tiers for Amazon Sponsored Ads, taken from BidX's own pricing page rather than a roundup. All figures are in euros and stated net of VAT.

  • Self Service — €495/month plus a percentage of connected ad spend. The platform, a growth manager, platform operations, and a monthly strategy call.
  • Managed Platform — €1,995/month plus a percentage. Adds up to two strategy calls a month and up to ten hours of assistance.
  • Managed Service — €4,995/month plus a percentage. Up to four strategy calls a month, up to 32 hours of assistance, full team support.

Amazon DSP is a separate line: €495/month self-service or €995/month managed, each plus a percentage. Amazon Marketing Cloud default reports are €295/month, with custom reports quoted on request. Share of Voice gives you the first 100 keywords free and then charges per additional 500 keywords — and that one is priced in dollars, at $250/month, on a page where everything else is in euros. Worth asking which currency lands on your invoice.

There is also a fourth product that the comparison posts almost never carry, because it lives on a separate landing page rather than the pricing page: BidX Essential, at $195/month for sellers managing up to $10K in monthly ad spend. It is the stripped-back version — monitoring and automated bidding, without the rest of the platform. If your spend is genuinely under that ceiling, it is the plan to ask about, and it changes the entry price of this vendor by a wide margin.

Two things the comparison posts get wrong

The currency. BidX is a German company and its pricing page quotes euros throughout. Plenty of third-party articles render the same tiers with dollar signs, which is not a rounding difference — it is a different number that moves with the exchange rate, on an annual commitment, before VAT. If you built a budget off a listicle, open the source page before you sign anything. BidX is not entirely consistent here either: the Essential landing page prices in dollars and refers to the entry platform tier in dollars too, while the pricing page has it in euros. Ask for the quote in your own currency, in writing.

The figure that keeps getting promoted. A monthly rate around the 295 mark circulates as though it were BidX's entry plan. On BidX's own page that number is €295 and it belongs to Amazon Marketing Cloud default reports — a reporting add-on, not a core plan, and not something you can buy instead of a platform tier. Two other figures that appear in roundups as BidX plan prices, in the low and mid thousands, do not correspond to anything the vendor publishes at all.

None of this is anybody behaving badly. Pricing pages change, add-ons get mistaken for plans, and roundups do not get re-checked. It is a good argument for reading the vendor's page yourself, including ours.

Three numbers to get in writing before you sign

1. The percentage. The variable rate on connected ad spend is not stated as a number anywhere on the pricing page. That is the figure that moves most as you scale, and it is the difference between a predictable software line and an open-ended one. Ask what it is, ask whether it tiers down at higher spend, and ask what counts as connected spend.

2. The term and the exit. Annual commitment, monthly payments — so ask what happens if you want out at month four, and whether the three-month paid proof of concept credits against the first year.

3. What is excluded. DSP, AMC reporting and Share of Voice are all priced separately, and prices are net of VAT. A quote for Sponsored Ads only is not a quote for your program. Add the modules you will actually turn on, apply your VAT position, then compare totals.

Where BidX is genuinely the right buy

This is a real product with real engineering behind it, and there are sellers who should buy it. If you have an in-house PPC person who wants a powerful console to drive, BidX gives them one. Its European coverage is a genuine strength — most US-built Amazon tools treat the EU marketplaces as an afterthought, and BidX does not.

The Managed Service tier is also honest about what it is. Four strategy calls and 32 hours of team time a month is people work at a people price, and the rate is inside the normal range for that. Judge that tier against agencies, not against software.

Where it gets harder is the middle. If you are paying €1,995 plus a percentage for ten hours a month of assistance, you are paying agency-adjacent money for tool-adjacent involvement. That is the tier worth pressure-testing.

Which one you should actually pick

BidX suits sellers with an in-house PPC owner, especially across European marketplaces, and its Managed Service tier is fairly priced for the team time it includes. Small accounts should ask about Essential rather than assume the platform tiers are the entry point. It is a harder sell if you want your all-in cost before a sales call, or if an annual commitment in euros is a problem. Dr. PPC publishes both halves of its price and runs month-to-month.

What to do with this

Before you compare subscription prices, pull your own search-term report for the last 90 days and total the spend against terms that produced no orders. On the account above that was 33.6% of everything spent. Whatever you buy — a seat, a service, or nothing — that number is the one it has to move, and a cheaper tool nobody has time to drive will not move it.

Common questions

How much does BidX cost per month?

BidX's published Sponsored Ads tiers are €495, €1,995 and €4,995 per month net of VAT, each plus an unstated percentage of connected ad spend. DSP, AMC reporting and Share of Voice are priced on top, and a smaller Essential plan is sold separately.

Is there a cheaper BidX plan for small sellers?

Yes, and it is easy to miss because it is not on the pricing page. BidX Essential is sold from its own landing page at $195/month for sellers managing up to $10K in monthly ad spend, covering performance monitoring and automated bidding rather than the full platform. Ask for it by name.

Why do other sites list a BidX plan around 295 a month?

Because an add-on has been mistaken for a plan. On BidX's own page that figure is €295 and it buys Amazon Marketing Cloud default reports, which sits alongside a platform tier rather than replacing one. The entry platform tier on that page is €495.

Does BidX have a free trial?

BidX's own page describes a three-month paid proof of concept for new customers. Some third-party articles mention a 14-day free trial. Confirm which applies to you before assuming a no-cost evaluation.

Is BidX worth it for a smaller seller?

The full platform starts at €495 a month plus a percentage on an annual commitment, which is a meaningful fixed cost for a small account. That is what the Essential plan exists for. Either way it makes most sense when someone in-house will actually use the console daily; if nobody owns PPC internally, you are buying a tool no one drives.
