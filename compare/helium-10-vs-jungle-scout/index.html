Helium 10 vs Jungle Scout: the short version

Both are Amazon seller software. Helium 10 and Jungle Scout handle product research, keyword tracking, and increasingly, PPC data. Helium 10 has pushed harder into ads automation and multi-marketplace support (Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop). Jungle Scout has stayed closer to its original strength: product research and a simpler workflow for sellers who haven't scaled into complex ad management yet.

The decision usually comes down to what job you need the software to do:

  • Need broad research plus PPC automation built into the platform: Helium 10 generally covers more ground at a comparable tier.
  • Need straightforward product research without a steep learning curve: Jungle Scout's workflow is easier to pick up on day one.
  • Need someone to actually execute and manage the PPC account, not just supply the dashboard: neither tool does that — that's the gap covered further down this page.

Where Helium 10 pulls ahead

Helium 10's product-research tools pull from Amazon's own Brand Analytics data — what shoppers actually searched, clicked, and bought — rather than relying only on keyword-demand modeling. That tends to produce research closer to real buyer behavior, particularly for sellers evaluating a new product idea.

It also covers more selling ground. Helium 10 supports Amazon, Walmart, and TikTok Shop from one platform. Jungle Scout is still primarily an Amazon tool. If you're already selling, or planning to sell, on more than one marketplace, that matters.

On the ads side, Helium 10 has built out bid rules, dayparting, and keyword harvesting inside its mid-tier plans. Jungle Scout offers PPC analytics and reporting at a comparable level, but similar automation has historically required stepping up to its enterprise tier. If you want the software itself to adjust bids and budgets, check exactly what's included at the plan you're paying for — the gap between reporting and automation is where sellers get surprised.

Where Jungle Scout pulls ahead

Jungle Scout earned its reputation the old-fashioned way: sales estimates, niche discovery, and a product-research workflow that thousands of private-label sellers have used to find their first winning product. For that specific job — find a product, size the opportunity, check the competition — it's a mature, well-understood tool.

It's also simpler to learn. Helium 10's feature set has grown wide: keyword research, rank tracking, PPC automation, listing tools, refund recovery, multi-marketplace dashboards. That breadth is an advantage if you need all of it. It's a liability if you're a new seller who just wants to find a product and check demand — Jungle Scout's narrower focus means less menu to get lost in.

If your business is pre-launch, or you're running a single Amazon storefront with no near-term plans to add Walmart or TikTok Shop, Jungle Scout's simplicity is a genuine reason to pick it over Helium 10 — not a consolation prize.

What neither tool actually does

Both platforms hand you data and controls. Neither one decides what to do with them. Someone still has to open the dashboard, read the report, decide the bid needs to move, make the change, and check back later to see if it worked. That someone is you, an employee, an agency, or nobody — and "nobody" is more common than sellers like to admit.

That's not a knock on either product. Research and reporting software isn't supposed to run your account for you. But it means the actual work of Amazon PPC — the daily decisions about what to raise, what to cut, and why — sits outside both tools, in whatever process you build around them.
