Teikametrics Pricing, and Where the Number Stops Being Public

Teikametrics Essentials is $179/month billed monthly or $149/month billed annually, for up to $10,000 in monthly ad spend. Above that, Advanced and Enterprise are custom-quoted, described only as plus 3% of ad spend over $10,000. Only the entry tier has a public price.

What Teikametrics publishes

Credit where it is due: Teikametrics puts a real number on the page, which more than half the platforms in this category will not do.

  • Essentials — $179/month monthly, $149/month annual. For sellers up to $10,000 a month in ad spend on Amazon or Walmart. Includes the ARI suite across ads, catalog, inventory and insights, plus refunds recovery. A free trial is offered.
  • Advanced — custom pricing, described as +3% of ad spend over $10K. Adds Amazon DSP and Walmart onsite display, integrated Amazon Marketing Cloud, dedicated onboarding, profitability dashboards, search dashboards, and the option to add managed services.
  • Enterprise — custom pricing, same 3%-over-$10K language, aimed at accounts over $100,000 a month in spend.

Both upper tiers route through a "free opportunity assessment" — a sales call — before you see a number.

The $10,000 threshold is the whole story

Everything about this price list turns on one line. Below $10,000 a month in ad spend you pay a flat $179 and you know exactly what next quarter looks like. Cross it, and you move onto a quoted base plus a variable that compounds with your own growth.

The published 3% is easy to model on the part they do disclose. At $30,000 a month in spend, 3% of the $20,000 above the threshold is $600 a month in variable fee. At $100,000, 3% of the $90,000 above it is $2,700 a month. At $250,000, it is $7,200. None of that includes the Advanced base fee, which is not published.

That is not a criticism of percentage pricing — we charge a percentage too. It is a warning about modelling. If you are near $10,000 and growing, price the tier you will be on in six months, not the one you qualify for today, because the upgrade conversation arrives on their timing, not yours.

About the numbers you will find on other sites

Several roundups report an Advanced base fee in the region of $1,199 to $1,430 a month. Teikametrics does not publish that figure, and we have not verified it against the vendor. Treat it as a directional third-party estimate and nothing more — it is exactly the kind of number that goes stale quietly and then gets copied for two years.

The same caution applies to the free trial. Teikametrics offers one on Essentials, but a trial of the entry tier tells you very little about the Advanced feature set you would actually be buying. Ask for a trial or a pilot of the tier you are being quoted on.

Two questions worth asking on the assessment call: is the 3% calculated on Amazon spend only or on Walmart too, and does the base fee change when you add a marketplace?

What Teikametrics is genuinely good at

Teikametrics has been in this category a long time and it shows in the breadth. Ads, catalog, inventory and insights sit in one place, which matters more than people expect — a bid decision made without inventory context is how you sell out of your best SKU in week three of Q4. Refunds recovery is a nice inclusion at the entry price.

Walmart support is a real differentiator. If Walmart is a serious channel for you rather than a rounding error, Teikametrics treats it as a first-class marketplace rather than a checkbox. Amazon Marketing Cloud access on the Advanced tier is also meaningful for brands ready to do real audience work.

If you are a hands-on operator under $10,000 a month in spend who wants a capable console and a predictable bill, Essentials at $149 to $179 is a fair, honest offer and you should consider it seriously.

Which one you should actually pick

Teikametrics Essentials is a genuinely fair deal for hands-on sellers under $10,000 a month in ad spend, and it is the better choice if Walmart matters to you. Above that threshold you are buying a quote, not a price. Dr. PPC suits brands that would rather not operate the console at all and want the full cost visible before the first call.

Common questions

How much does Teikametrics cost?

Essentials is $179/month billed monthly or $149/month billed annually for up to $10,000 in monthly ad spend. Advanced and Enterprise are custom-quoted and add 3% of ad spend above $10,000.

What is the Teikametrics 3% fee calculated on?

Their pricing page describes it as 3% of ad spend over $10,000 per month, so the first $10,000 is covered by the base fee. Confirm on the call whether Walmart spend is included in that calculation.

Does Teikametrics offer a free trial?

Yes, on the Essentials plan. There is no published trial for Advanced or Enterprise — those route through a sales assessment. Ask for a pilot of the tier you are actually being quoted.

Is the Advanced plan really around $1,200 a month?

That range appears on several third-party sites. Teikametrics does not publish it and we have not confirmed it with the vendor. Get the figure in writing from Teikametrics rather than budgeting from a roundup.

Which is cheaper at $50,000 a month in ad spend?

You cannot know, because the Teikametrics Advanced base fee is not public — only the 3%-over-$10K component is. Dr. PPC at that spend is $300 plus 3%, capped, and both numbers are on the page. That transparency is the comparison, not the arithmetic.
