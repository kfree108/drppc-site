Operating principles behind Dr. PPC’s research

Every guide, calculator, and benchmark published on drppc.ai follows four operator rules drawn directly from live managed accounts:

  1. Margin before vanity ACoS: Every bid ceiling starts from true unit economics (price, COGS, freight, FBA fees, referral fee, storage, and returns) and tracks TACoS alongside profit dollars.
  2. Complete attribution windows only: Never cut a keyword or judge a bid move on incomplete days still filling in from 7-day or 14-day attribution lag.
  3. Statistical patience on negatives: Give search terms 3× clicks per order before negating, so average winners are not killed by small-sample noise.
  4. Plain-English evidence and rollback triggers: Every ad change carries a written reason, a measurement window, and a one-click rollback trigger.
