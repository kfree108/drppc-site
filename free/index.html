Free Amazon PPC tools

Updated 2026-10-22 · free, no sign-up · runs in your browser
How it works

Five small tools for the five rules in our short videos. Each one runs in your browser, with no sign-up. Put in your own numbers and they work out the rest.

  • The 3× TableFree table: how many clicks a search term gets before you negate it, for every conversion rate from 1% to 40%. Three times clicks per order is about 95% sure.
  • The 3-Number Bid CalculatorFree Amazon PPC bid calculator: price × target ACoS × conversion rate = your max bid. Works out break-even ACoS and the base bid after a top-of-search boost.
  • The Bid Stack CalculatorFree calculator: base bid × placement boost × dynamic bidding = the most an Amazon Sponsored Products click can cost on top of search, rest of search and product pages.
  • The Two-Lane SorterFree tool: drop in your Sponsored Products search term report and your brand name, and see branded vs non-branded ACoS side by side, so a healthy blended ACoS can't hide your expensive campaigns.
  • The Date Windows SheetFree sheet: Amazon credits a Sponsored Products sale for 7 days after the click and dates it to the click, so recent days always look worse. Here are the date windows to use for every report.

How to use it

  1. Pick the tool for the decision you’re making.
  2. Put in your own numbers.
  3. Download the table if you want to keep it.

Good to know

  • Nothing you type or upload leaves your browser.
